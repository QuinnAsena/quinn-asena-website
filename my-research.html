My Research

Ten peer-reviewed publications with DOIs, plus plain-language summaries of selected papers on palaeoecology, statistical ecology and climate justice.

My research is about ecological change: what drives it, how fast it happens, and how confidently we can detect it. That has taken me from fossil pollen records spanning millennia to continental-scale simulations of boreal forests under future climates.

If you can’t access any of the full articles, get in touch and I’ll send you a copy. The same record with citation counts is on Google Scholar and ORCID, and everything I write is open on GitHub.
